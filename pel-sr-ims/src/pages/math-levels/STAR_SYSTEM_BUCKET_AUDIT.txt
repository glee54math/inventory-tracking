================================================================================
THE NEW FORMULA (now shared — see "CODE STATUS" below)
================================================================================
Lives in src/hooks/useModuleProgress.ts as two exported functions:

  isMastered(goal, requiredBuckets, p)
  starCount(goal, requiredBuckets, p)

For a unit with NO requiredBuckets: unchanged old behavior — stars = clean
solve count, capped at `goal`.

For a unit WITH requiredBuckets: stars = Math.ceil(goal * coveredCount /
totalBuckets), capped at goal-1 UNTIL every bucket is covered, at which
point it jumps to the full `goal`. (Worked example, 9 buckets, goal=5:
1 bucket->1 star, 2->2, 3->2, 4->3, 5->3, 6->4, 7->4, 8->4 [would
naturally ceil to 5, but held back], 9/9->5.)

A "bucket" = one scenario-class a problem generator can produce, declared
via Problem.bucket on each generated problem and Unit.requiredBuckets on
the unit as a whole (see shared/engine/types.ts and shared/units/types.ts
doc comments). "Clean solve" = solved with at most one hint.

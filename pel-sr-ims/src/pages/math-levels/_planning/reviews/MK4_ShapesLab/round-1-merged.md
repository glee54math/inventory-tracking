## Proposed rule and guideline changes (wait for Stage 6)

From the curriculum report:
1. A finished step's `explain`, answer chip and custom `resultText` must not state a later step's answer (MK4; all grades). *Also proposed by the English checker for checklist §3.*
2. Color must not sort the answer classes (MK4; all grades).
3. Teach the idea, not a drawing cue such as shading (MK4 U2; K–2, then all grades).
4. A custom "build" step must allow a wrong choice (MK4 U4; all grades).
5. Keep shapes inside the picture at the largest scale and worst turn (MK4; all grades).

From the English report:
6. checklist §3 Giveaways: finished step's chip/explain (same as 1).
7. math-language-traps §8: a slash meaning "trade places" is read as "or"; a spaced slash is silent (MK4; all grades).
8. math-language-traps §8: no 's on an all-caps word (MK4; all grades).
9. grade-K Math words: *turn* is the one word for rotating; *flip* is a different move (MK4; K–1).
10. grade-K Watch for: *side* has one meaning per lab (MK4; K–1).
11. grade-K What they can read: every K–1 Test question needs a picture (MK4; K–1).

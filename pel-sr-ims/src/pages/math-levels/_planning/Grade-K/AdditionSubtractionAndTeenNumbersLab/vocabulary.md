## Findings

1. **"=" is still not explained in K.** It is read aloud as "equals" beside pictures from Unit 1. MG1 Unit 6 teaches "the same as". Not changed in round 1.
2. **"How many more" appears only in the optional Unit 2 Challenge** (user decision D1). A later lab can't assume it was learned here; MG1 Unit 4 teaches compare stories.
3. **MK1's list still ends with the stray tag** noted in E1-47. It is out of scope for this lab's fixes.

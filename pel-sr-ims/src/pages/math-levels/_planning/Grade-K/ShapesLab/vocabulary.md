## Findings

1. **"Stick" means a side here and one ten in MG2.** Kept by user decision (E1-36, 2026-11-25).
2. **"Box"** is an orange box in the Unit 1 scenes, next to MK2/MG1's ten-frame "boxes". Picture-supported, low risk.
3. **"Flat (2D)" / "Solid (3D)"** stay on buttons and prompts (user decision); the voice reads "Flat 2D". "2D/3D" is not taught as a word beyond the labels.
4. **"Diamond"** is used only as a name some people give a turned square (Explore line), and as a wrong choice.

## Findings

1. **"Tall" means two different things.** CFU: "Tall tells how high something is." (`DescribingHowObjectsAreMeasured.tsx:73`). Explore, Practice and Test file *tall* under length (`WORDS` `:46–52`; Explore "'tall' describes length" `:196`; vocabMatch Practice `:300–318`, Test `DescribingHowObjectsAreMeasuredTest.tsx:98–114`). A student who learned the CFU line answers "not length". **Fix:** drop *tall* from `WORDS`, or add a height attribute; the K words to keep are long/short, heavy/light.
2. **Both meanings of "left" in one lab.** Left/Right balance sides (`ComparingTwoObjectsDirectly.tsx:148–155`, Explore `:215–216`, Practice `:297–308`, Test) and "left to sort / left over" (`ClassifyingAndCountingByCategory.tsx:262, :424`). grade-K.md: don't use both in one lab. **Fix:** name the pans by color or by what's on them ("the rock side"), or say "still to sort" / "the extras".
3. **Grade-K not-yet word needed to answer:** "What attribute were these items sorted by?" (`ClassifyingAndCountingByCategory.tsx:312`) and *attribute* in Explore/Key Ideas (`DescribingHowObjectsAreMeasured.tsx:183, :335`; `ComparingTwoObjectsDirectly.tsx:354`; `ClassifyingAndCountingByCategory.tsx:437`). **Fix:** "How were these sorted? Color or shape?"
4. **Shape names before MK4.** Unit 3 sorts *circles / squares / triangles* (`ClassifyingAndCountingByCategory.tsx:82–83, :156–166, :218, :276`); MK4 Unit 1 teaches those names. Not a hard lock (labs at one level aren't ordered), but MK3 is earlier. **Fix:** put a shape picture on each bin label and in the voice ("goes with the round ones"), or sort Unit 3 by color/size only.
5. **Test-only words:** *opposite* (`DescribingHowObjectsAreMeasuredTest.tsx:192, :207`), *pan / sinks* (`ComparingTwoObjectsDirectlyTest.tsx:201`), *fewest* (`ClassifyingAndCountingByCategoryTest.tsx:229`). **Fix:** "Which word goes with long: short or light?"; "The heavy side goes… up or down?"; "Which group has the least? / Which group runs out first?" — or teach each in Practice first.
6. **"Subtract" untaught; hyphen as minus.** `ClassifyingAndCountingByCategory.tsx:424`, Test `:141`, show-me `"${moreCount} - ${fewerCount}"` (`:424`, Test `:142`; also `ComparingTwoObjectsDirectlyTest.tsx:147, :170`). **Fix:** "Match them up. Count the extras." (as MK1/MG1 do), and use "−" if an equation stays.
7. **Several words for one idea:** *weight / heavy / heavier*, fine; but *rod*, *train*, *bar* (LengthBars) for the length picture, and *side / pan* for one half of the balance. **Fix:** one name per picture (Rod; side).
8. **True/false in every unit** (Unit 1 Test `:139, :159`; Unit 2 Practice `:286`, Test `:95, :184`; Unit 3 Practice `:342`, Test `:157`). **Fix:** one new picture example per item.

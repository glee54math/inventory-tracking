## Words this lab avoids on purpose

| Avoided | Used instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| attribute, measurable, category, classifying (in student sentences) | how long, how heavy, group, sort | Not-yet words at K |
| left, right | Blue side, Orange side | Untaught, and "left" also means "still there" |
| circled, item | this one, with a ring and an arrow | "Circled" sounds like "circle" |
| balance, pan, tips, sinks | scale, goes down, goes up | One word per idea |
| how many more, how many longer, subtract, − and = | How many extra? Count the ringed extras | Compare problems are not yet K (D3) |
| left over, left to sort | Sort 3 more. Count the extras. | Same reason as left |
| true or false, opposite, describes | A new picture example | Not yet at K |
| actually, even though, no matter | Short separate sentences | Too hard by ear |
| click | tap | Students are on iPads |
| rule | sort the same way | No picture, and at home it means "what you are allowed to do" (E2-54) |
| smallest, biggest (for groups) | fewest, most | One word per idea (E2-53) |
| runs out | stops first | Phrasal verb (E2-59) |

1. **Shape names come before MK4.** Unit 3 uses them with a shape picture on every label and button. Not a hard lock (labs at one level aren't ordered).
2. **"Rule" is gone from student text** (round 2, E2-54). Internal code names keep it.

# High School (MH1-6, MHG, MHT) — Deferred

Scope for this planning round was K-8 only. High school was deliberately
left out of detailed planning and stubbed here instead, per the scope
decision made when this `_planning/` structure was created.

## Why deferred
The CA CCSSM higher-mathematics standards are organized very differently
from K-8: two possible course pathways (Traditional: Algebra I / Geometry
/ Algebra II, vs. Integrated: Math I / II / III), plus Advanced Placement
Probability & Statistics and Calculus, all layered on top of six
conceptual categories (Number & Quantity, Algebra, Functions, Modeling,
Geometry, Statistics & Probability) that cut across course boundaries.
Committing to a course-to-slot mapping is a bigger judgment call than any
single K-8 grade's domain breakdown, and grade 8 already competes for the
`MH1` slot (see `Grade-8/README` notes in the index) — that overlap should
get resolved before high school's own detailed planning starts, not
guessed at here.

## Slot inventory (for reference only — no content planned yet)
- `MH1` — grade 9 / Algebra I or Math I (also the slot grade-8 content may
  partially compete for; see `Grade-8` inventory)
- `MH2` — grade 9 (second slot)
- `MH3` — grade 10 / Geometry or Math II
- `MHG` — grade 10 (second slot; name suggests Geometry-specific)
- `MH4` — grade 11 / Algebra II or Math III
- `MHT` — grade 11 (second slot; name suggests Trigonometry-specific)
- `MH5` — grade 12 (Advanced Placement Probability & Statistics track)
- `MH6` — grade 12 (Calculus track)

## Before planning this round
1. Decide Traditional vs. Integrated pathway (or whether to offer both,
   mapped to separate slots) — a genuine curriculum-sequencing choice,
   not something to default silently.
2. Resolve the `MH1` grade-8/grade-9 overlap once Grade 8's labs
   (`_planning/Grade-8/`) are actually built and their slot is confirmed.
3. Repeat the Stage-0 planning process used for K-8 (see
   `_planning/README.md`) once the above is decided.
